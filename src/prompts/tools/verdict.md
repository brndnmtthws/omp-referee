Record your ruling on the current review. Call this tool once per review, after inspecting the project. `pass` ends the agent's run, so be certain.

- `verdict`: `pass` or `fail` for a completion claim; `escalate` or `fail` for a blocked claim.
- `summary`: at least 30 characters stating what you checked, how, and why it supports the ruling.
- `findings`: required for `fail`; one entry per unmet requirement naming the requirement, the evidence, and what must change.
