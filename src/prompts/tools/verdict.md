Record your ruling on the current review. Call it exactly once per review, after investigating.

- `verdict`: for a completion claim, `pass` or `fail`; for a blocked claim, `escalate` or `fail`.
- `summary`: at least 30 characters: what you checked, how, and why it supports the ruling.
- `findings`: required for `fail`: one entry per unmet requirement, naming what is wrong, the evidence, and what must change.

`pass` ends the agent's run: be certain.
